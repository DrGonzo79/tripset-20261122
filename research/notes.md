Derived from the free, rendered **IdeaBrowser Idea of the Day** report for **November 22, 2026**: “SaaS tool that adapts prescribed workouts to hotel gym equipment for traveling strength clients.” Public source: https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today. ## Report signal

- IB Index **6.5**; category SaaS; modeled ceiling **$2M–$4M ARR**; moderate difficulty; **$3K–$15K** starting capital; first revenue **60–90 days**.
- Buyer: initially independent strength coaches with 10–50 remote clients paying $200–$500/month, but the report's pivot makes the traveling lifter the lead buyer.
- Painful status quo: clients encounter unknown hotel equipment, improvise badly, or text coaches after hours; coaches manually rewrite sessions or send generic bodyweight circuits.
- Gap/wedge: Everfit and Trainerize already support coach-configured alternates. The narrower opening is translating a hotel's actual inventory while preserving the coach's program intent and escalating unsafe gaps.
- Why now: reported 133× vision-cost decline since 2024, $423B projected 2026 US business travel, and 48% of coaches reportedly operating hybrid models.
- Demand signals: 1,590 monthly US searches across “hotel room workout” and “hotel gym workout”; repeated coach/traveler pain statements; Fitbod estimated near $1M/month iOS revenue and 170K downloads/month. These are proxies, not proof of this offer.
- Competitors: Everfit, Trainerize, Fitbod, Gymverse, TrueCoach, TrainHeroic, Adapt Fitness, FitTraveler and TravelTone. Incumbents own substitution libraries; no durable data moat exists on day one.
- Offer ladder: free Hotel Gym Decoder; $29 one-trip adaptation; $9.99/month frequent-traveler plan; $19–$49/month flat roster-priced coach white-label.
- Economics in report: conservative year one $15K–$25K; optimistic $60K–$120K; modeled month-12 blended MRR about $2,500. Most inputs are assumptions.
- GTM: traveler SEO and free photo decoder first; community participation second. Coach-side search is near zero, so broad cold outreach is weak.
- Biggest risks: Everfit/Trainerize already ship adjacent functionality; Fitbod could import coached programs; seasonal churn; automated substitution safety/liability; weak coach-side search demand.
- Public source domains cited in the report include aicostcheck.com, GBTA/Travel and Tour World, Deloitte, trainerize.com, NASM, Reddit, TurnFit, App Store estimates, TrueCoach and TrainHeroic.

## Build implication

The prototype proves the defensible interaction: select confirmed equipment, apply explicit coach-authored rules, preserve movement intent, and stop for coach review when no rule fits. Photo recognition and AI are labeled simulated because neither is necessary to test the core value.
